The user pressed a "Show me something cool" button — they want to be
impressed but haven't said by what. Do NOT start building yet.

First, ask one question eliciting what they'd like to see
(immediately — no research needed): post a concise numbered list in
chat with 4-5 enticing, concrete directions as options — e.g.
"A generative art piece", "A playable mini-game", "A slick product
concept", "An interactive data visualization" — plus a "surprise me /
decide for me" option, which means: pick the direction YOU can execute
most impressively. Invite a free-text steer if it's worth having — one
round, no second interview. End your turn and wait for the reply.

Once they answer, build the most polished, visually striking version
you can — favor motion, interactivity, and craft over breadth.
